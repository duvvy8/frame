Downloads

There is no download manager yet. Frame has no CefDownloadHandler, which means a download is currently declined rather than saved somewhere you cannot see.

  • Where Will ask, or use the system Downloads folder — decided once, visibly, rather than silently.
  • Progress Will live here and in the sidebar, not in a floating shelf.
  • Incognito Files are files; they persist. Only the record of them will not.
